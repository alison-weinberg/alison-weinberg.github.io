Selected Work

Branching Scenario · Articulate Storyline 360

Branching Investigation Module

A fully custom scenario-based module built around a 75+ variable system that drives map gating, NPC dialogue, delayed content delivery, and branching progression. Includes a persistent inbox (custom JavaScript/localStorage bridge), adjustable dual language modes for differentiated instruction without separate builds, and a scored debrief that assesses reasoning over task completion. Architecture designed for choice and consequence rather than forced linear paths. Applied to a secondary ELA curriculum (Shakespeare's Romeo and Juliet) as proof of concept; the underlying system is content-agnostic.

Sales Enablement · Articulate Storyline 360

Sales Scenario: Probing & Recommendation

A linear sales scenario built around a sequenced pair of interactions. A slider-based probing-questions exercise, using custom JavaScript to cap selections at three, contrasts open- and closed-ended questions and requires a target score before advancing. The next interaction, an active-listening hotspot exercise, displays the customer's response on screen so learners identify phrases that align with specific product features. A final interaction asks learners to recommend the product whose features best match the customer's stated needs and preferences. Storefront and product set are original (AI-generated) to remove proprietary branding from the source project.

Software Simulation · Articulate Storyline 360

Salesforce Navigation Sim

A Salesforce systems simulation built for call center agents assisting healthcare providers with billing and account questions. Rather than a slide-per-tab walkthrough, a single hotspot interaction replicates the live record environment: clicking each tab dynamically reveals that section's information, with a callout explaining what it contains and when an agent would need it (verifying a provider's address, looking up a claim, confirming network status and affiliated groups). Five multiple-choice questions assess retention of provider type concepts and record navigation. Designed for new-hire onboarding, where accurate, efficient navigation supports both caller assistance and regulatory compliance.

Storyboard Portfolio

Three storyboards from SME-collaborative projects, approved by clients and handed to development teams for build. Demonstrates instructional design at the planning stage: scoping interactions, structuring content sequences, and translating subject matter expertise into buildable specifications.

  • Opportunity Selling — a click-to-explore office/manufacturing floor scenario where learners identify unspoken customer needs, with each discovery moving a sales-impact dial. An earlier version of the interaction pattern used in the Sales Scenario module above.
  • S.A.F.E. Driving — a safety module using a 360°-rotation truck interaction to call out pre-drive and backing point-checks alongside broader safety reminders (seatbelts, clearance, cab cleanliness).
  • Phases of Construction — a jobsite module introducing roles (foreman, GC, etc.) and decision-maker vs. end-user dynamics, foundational to a course series building toward trade-specific tool recommendations.
